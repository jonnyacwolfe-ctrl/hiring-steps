British Columbia

Employer

  • Registered in BC, with an office and a business licence there
  • Operating in BC 1+ year
  • 3+ full-time staff in BC (5+ in Metro Vancouver)
  • No government fines in the last 2 years

Job

  • Full-time (30+ h/week), permanent, skilled (engineer, R&D lead, manager…)
  • Pay in the normal range for the role
  • Can be home-based in BC
Shortcut: pay ≥ CAD 49/h and ≥ $99,000/yr. BC’s latest draw (29 Oct 2026) invited every such job, no points needed.

Steps

  1. Post the job on 2 job sites (e.g. Job Bank, Indeed) for 14+ days.
  2. Sign an offer letter.
  3. Give the candidate: signed Employer Declaration Form, recommendation letter, job description, company info, certificate of incorporation, business licence.
  4. If his job changes before he gets PR, email PNPInfo@gov.bc.ca within 30 days.

He files everything else.

Newfoundland & Labrador

Employer

  • NL business, 2+ years under current owners
  • 2+ full-time local staff (St. John’s area) or 1+ elsewhere
  • WorkplaceNL and Labour Standards clearances

Job

  • Full-time (30+ h/week), at least 2 years, skilled
  • Salary or hourly at the local going rate
  • Based in NL (remote OK if in NL)
Shortcut: titled Mechanical, Biomedical or Electrical Engineer, or Research Associate (tech sector) = no job ads, priority processing.

Steps

  1. Unless the shortcut applies: advertise 3+ weeks on Job Bank (with Job Match) plus one other site, right before step 2. Note how many applied and were interviewed.
  2. Apply for a Job Vacancy Assessment (free) in Immigration Accelerator; when approved, invite the candidate there.
  3. Sign an employment contract and the NLPNP Employment Offer form (signed by someone with HR and budget authority).

He files everything else.

Alberta

Harder than BC or Newfoundland. 2026 places are almost used up; next round 2027.

Employer

  • Alberta company in a tech industry: engineering services, R&D, medical or control instruments, machinery, electronics, aerospace, software…
  • 2+ full fiscal years in Alberta
  • $400,000+ revenue last year, 3+ full-time staff in Alberta

Job

  • Full-time (30+ h/week), 12+ months, worked in Alberta
  • A listed role: engineering manager, mechanical or electrical engineer, engineering technologist…
  • Pay at least the usual starting wage for the role in Alberta
  • No “engineer” in his title, and engineering work under a licensed engineer
Best odds: job outside Calgary and Edmonton, ideally in a Rural Renewal community that endorses him.

Steps

  1. Advertise the job in Canada first.
  2. Sign a job offer.

He files everything else.
